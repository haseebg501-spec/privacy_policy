# Privacy Policy for EMS Admission Form Extractor

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy applies to the **EMS Admission Form Extractor** Chrome Extension ("the Extension"). The Extension is designed as an internal administrative tool exclusively for PGC (Punjab Group of Colleges) campuses to streamline and manage admission entries. 

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled securely and responsibly. This policy outlines what data we collect, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide secure access and maintain the integrity of the admission entry process, the Extension collects the following strictly necessary information during your active session:
* **Name:** To identify the authorized staff member processing the admission.
* **Email Address:** To verify your identity and authorization level within the PGC system.
* **Session Login Time:** To maintain system security, audit logs, and manage active session timeouts.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
The data collected is used strictly for operational and security purposes related to the Extension's core functionality. Specifically, we use your data to:
* Authenticate your identity before allowing access to the admission form extraction features.
* Ensure that only authorized PGC campus personnel are interacting with the admission system.
* Maintain an internal audit trail of when admission entries were processed.

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure
We respect your privacy and the confidentiality of institutional data. 
* **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your name, email, or session data with any third parties, advertisers, or external organizations. 
* **No Unethical Use:** Your data will never be used for marketing purposes or any activity outside the explicit scope of managing PGC admission entries.

## 5. Data Security
We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of the data collected during your session. The Extension only communicates with secure, authorized backend servers to validate your session.

## 6. Data Retention
The Extension only retains your session data (Name, Email, Login time) for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, typically for the duration of your active login session and for standard institutional security logging.

## 7. User Rights
As a user, you have the right to:
* Know what data is being collected (as outlined in this policy).
* Log out of the Extension at any time to terminate the active session and halt ongoing data collection.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be updated on this page, and the "Effective Date" at the top will be revised accordingly.
